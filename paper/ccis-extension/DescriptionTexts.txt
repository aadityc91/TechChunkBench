1 (Fig. 1) - Three panels of horizontal stacked bars, one per corpus regime, showing for each of six retrieval metrics how total variance divides between chunking strategy, chunk size, embedding model, corpus and residual. The chunking-strategy segment is hatched and labelled; it is 1 to 2 percent in general QA, 4.5 to 25 percent in technical documentation, and below 1 percent in legal contracts.
2 (Fig. 2) - Scatter plot of 28 corpora. Horizontal axis: Markdown headings per ten thousand characters, from 0 to 15. Vertical axis: within-corpus share of MRR variance explained by chunking strategy, from 0 to 65 percent. Technical documents are circles and rise with heading density; the four legal sub-benchmarks are squares clustered at zero heading density and low variance share.
3 (Fig. 3) - Two bar charts of mean MRR for three conditions: fixed-size without prefix, structural boundaries without prefix, structural boundaries with heading prefix. Left panel, technical documents: bars rise from 0.588 to 0.634 to 0.654. Right panel, legal contracts with the legal-aware parser: bars fall from 0.244 to 0.218 and recover slightly to 0.220.
