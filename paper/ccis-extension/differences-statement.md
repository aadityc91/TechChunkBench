# Statement of differences between the conference version and the extended version
(for the PRIMORIS submission form; INSTICC requirement 6)

The conference paper (Chauhan and Hegde, DATA 2026, "Chunking Is Not the Bottleneck") reported a 32-configuration factorial experiment on Natural Questions and MS-MARCO showing that chunking strategy explains only 1–2% of retrieval-quality variance, a 2×2 heading-prefix ablation on Natural Questions, and an end-to-end LLM-judge evaluation. The extended version keeps that experiment only as a cited baseline (regime R1) and asks the follow-up question the conference paper left open: under what corpus conditions does chunking matter? It adds two new experimental regimes run with the same design and a third embedding model: 24 structured technical documents across eight domains with exact evidence-span labels (576 configurations), and the four LegalBench-RAG legal-contract benchmarks with expert-annotated gold spans (144 configurations including two new structure-aware variants). It further adds analyses that did not exist in the conference version: strategy-by-regime and strategy-by-corpus interaction terms, bootstrap confidence intervals on effect sizes, Holm-corrected significance, paired Cliff's delta contrasts, a corpus-feature regression identifying heading density as the predictor of the chunking effect, a floor-effect check for the legal regime, a replication of the boundary-versus-metadata ablation on both new regimes, a legal-aware structure parser motivated by the finding that generic structure-aware chunkers detect no structure in contracts, a controlled comparison of fuzzy versus exact relevance labels on identical retrieval runs, and a cost analysis.

The central finding is new: chunking's share of variance is non-monotonic across regimes, rising to 5–8% and high significance on technical documentation and falling below 1% on long legal contracts, which refutes the conference paper's own prediction that long documents would be where chunking matters most. The title, abstract, introduction, related work (about 30 new references from 2024–2026), discussion, implications and conclusions have been rewritten around this question; the methods section describes the shared design once and the three regimes separately. The only material carried over is the summary of the R1 experiment and its η² values, reproduced in one table column with citation.
